Montenegro
🧳 Smart Route via Dubrovnik · No direct flights, worth every minute of driving
The Route

There's no direct flight from Dublin to Montenegro — but that's exactly why most Irish travellers miss out on one of Europe's most underrated countries. Fly into Dubrovnik, rent a cheap car at the airport, and 30-40 minutes of coastal driving later you're crossing the border at Herceg Novi. From there, the Bay of Kotor opens up — and it's one of the most beautiful drives in Europe.

Must see · Kotor & Perast
🏘️
Kotor Old Town
Medieval walled town inside a stunning fjord-like bay
🐍
Serpentines of Kotor
Legendary hairpin road climbing the mountain — drive it yourself
🍹
Horizont Bar
Up in the mountains above Kotor — the entire bay laid out beneath you. One of the best views in the Balkans.
⛪
Perast
Tiny baroque town next to Kotor — boat out to Our Lady of the Rocks islet
Where we ate · Kotor
🥪
XL Sandwich
Exactly what the name says — huge, excellent sandwiches. Great cheap lunch.
🍝
Spaghetti and Wine Bar
Properly good Italian food at very fair prices — a real find in Kotor.
Beyond Kotor
🚤
Lake Skadar boat tour
Around €15 for a boat tour of the largest lake in Southern Europe — stunning and peaceful
🏝️
Sveti Stefan
Iconic islet resort — one of the most photographed views in the Adriatic
🫒
Restaurant Olive
Right by Sveti Stefan — superb view, relaxed chill atmosphere. Perfect spot to slow down.
Practical tips
🚗
Rent in Dubrovnik: car hire is cheap and the drive itself is part of the experience.
🛂
Border crossing: straightforward with an Irish passport, usually just a few minutes.
🐍
Serpentines: not for nervous drivers, but the views from the top are unforgettable.
📅
Best time: May-June or September — warm, swimmable, without peak summer crowds.
HOW TO GET THERE
Fly Dublin → Dubrovnik · 🚗 Rent a car at the airport · 🛂 Drive 30-40min to the border at Herceg Novi · 📍 Continue to Kotor (20min further)
